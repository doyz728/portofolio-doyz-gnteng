About Me

Who is G-DEV

Hello! I am a passionate Frontend Developer based in Jakarta. With a strong background in Computer Science, I love bridging the gap between engineering and design.

My journey is driven by a curiosity to create digital experiences that are not only functional but also visually stunning and user-friendly. I specialize in building responsive websites using modern technologies.

When I'm not coding, you can find me exploring new design trends, contributing to open source, or enjoying a good cup of coffee.
